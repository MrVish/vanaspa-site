# VĀNA — WhatsApp Business scripts

To set these up: WhatsApp Business → Settings → Business tools. Add each script under **Quick replies** with the
shortcut shown (type `/` in a chat to pick one). Swap anything in `[brackets]` before sending.

Replace `[REVIEW LINK]` everywhere with your Google review link. To find it, open your Business Profile on Google,
choose **Ask for reviews**, and copy the link.

> Hi [Name], looking forward to seeing you tomorrow at [time]. Three quick questions so your therapist is ready:
> 1. Where do you feel it most — or what would you like us to focus on?
> 2. How firm do you like the pressure: light, medium or firm?
> 3. Anything we should avoid or know about — injuries, pregnancy, blood pressure, allergies, a bad night's sleep?
> Just reply here — two minutes, and the hour is all yours.

### `/reminder` — Same-day reminder (optional, 2–3 hours before)
> See you at [time] today, [Name]. Come ten minutes early — parking is on site at E Tower. If anything changes, just
> reply here.

## Rules for the team

- **Review requests:** ask every guest, not just the happy ones. Never offer a discount or freebie for a review,
  and never suggest what to write. Google doesn't allow either.
- **Broadcasts:** send only to guests who agreed to hear from you, at most once a month.
- **Labels** (Business tools → Labels): *New enquiry* · *Booked* · *Visited* · *Review asked* · *Reviewed* ·
  *Member*. Check *New enquiry* every evening and follow up on anyone who asked but didn't book.
- **"How did you hear about us?"** Ask at every first booking, and note the answer in the bookings sheet.
